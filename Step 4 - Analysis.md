# Step 4 - Analysis

This section answers six business questions using the star schema, joining fact_table to its dimensions. 
Because Step 1 showed the dimensions have no duplicate keys and no orphaned rows, the joins cannot inflate totals. 
Every result is reconciled back to the source totals (sales of 2,297,200.86 and profit of 286,397.02), and every margin is total profit divided by total sales, as defined in Step 3. 
Each finding gives the number, what it means and a recommendation.
